What is AnimoLabs?

AnimoLabs is a web-based computer lab reservation system developed for De La Salle University. Students can view available seats, reserve time slots, and manage their bookings. Lab technicians have a dedicated dashboard to oversee all reservations and manage lab availability.

The system follows the Model-View-Controller (MVC) architecture, using MongoDB for data persistence, Express.js for server-side routing, and vanilla HTML/CSS/JS for the front-end.
