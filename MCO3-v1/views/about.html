What is AnimoLabs?

AnimoLabs is a web-based computer lab reservation system developed for De La Salle University. Students can view available seats, reserve time slots, and manage their bookings. Lab technicians can oversee reservations and manage lab availability.

The system follows the Model-View-Controller (MVC) architecture, using MongoDB, Express.js, and HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.
